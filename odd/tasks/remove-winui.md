# Feature: Remove the legacy WinUI (MeetingLive) app from the repo

Locator: `odd/tasks/remove-winui.md` · Engram mirror: `odd/remove-winui/tasks` · Branch: `chore/remove-winui`

## Objective
Drop the unused WinUI app (`MeetingLive.App/`, `MeetingLive.Core/`, `MeetingLive.Core.Tests/`, `MeetingLive.sln`; 424 tracked files) now that the product is the Electron app Vozella (rename `dd5658f`).

## Constraint found
`apps/desktop` strings `en.json` / `es.json` are generated by `scripts/convert-strings.mjs` from `MeetingLive.App/Strings/*/Resources.resw` and `MeetingLive.Core/Strings/CoreStrings*.resx`. Those JSON files must become the hand-edited source first.

## Kept on purpose (user agreed 2026-11-07)
- Legacy data-folder detection (`LEGACY_FOLDER_NAME = 'MeetingLive'`, `sharesMeetingLive`) and the forbidden-real-data guard.
- The internal meeting link scheme `https://meetinglive.local/meeting/` (stored in chats/notes).
